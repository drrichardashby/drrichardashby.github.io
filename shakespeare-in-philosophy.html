Shakespeare in Philosophy (ShiP)

I am on the organising committee of Shakespeare in Philosophy (ShiP), a non-profit public symposium series created by Richard Wilson, Sir Peter Hall Professor Emeritus of Shakespeare Studies at Kingston University. We host events exploring the relation between Shakespeare and philosophy. Our most recent symposia were on ‘Shakespeare and the Slovenian School of Philosophy and Psychoanalysis’ (2025) and ‘Shakespeare and British Inter-war Philosophy’ (2026).

ShiP events are held at Garrick’s Temple to Shakespeare on the banks of the River Thames. These events are a direct continuation of the ‘Shakespeare at the Temple’ symposia organised at Garrick’s Temple to Shakespeare originally under Richard Wilson’s Kingston Shakespeare Seminar (2013–2019).
