Teaching and Supervision

Undergraduate and postgraduate teaching and supervision in Shakespeare, early modern literature, theatre, adaptation and twentieth- and twenty-first-century culture.

King’s College London

Teaching has included Shakespeare’s London, Early Modern Comedy and Identity, A Mad World My Masters: Performing Jacobean Culture, Theatre and Politics in the Twentieth and Twenty-First Centuries, Film of the Play, Premodern Race and Gender, and a third-year module on Shakespeare and Holocaust Writing: Testimony, Literature, Philosophy.

Royal Holloway & elsewhere

Teaching at Royal Holloway has included Shakespeare, Renaissance literature, early modern bodies, poetry, adaptation and Shakespeare on screen, alongside postgraduate teaching and dissertation supervision.

I have also contributed invited teaching and supervision at other institutions.
