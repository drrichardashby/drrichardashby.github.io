Teaching and Supervision

Teaching and Supervision

Undergraduate and postgraduate teaching and supervision in Shakespeare, early modern literature, post-war and contemporary theatre, adaptation and appropriation, Holocaust writing, and twentieth- and twenty-first-century literary culture.

King’s College London

Teaching has included “Shakespeare in London”, “Early Modern Comedy and Identity”, “A Mad World My Masters: Performing Jacobean Culture”, “Theatre and Politics in the Twentieth and Twenty-First Centuries”, “Film of the Play”, “Premodern Race and Gender”, and a third-year module on “Shakespeare and Holocaust Writing: Testimony, Literature, Philosophy”.

Royal Holloway & elsewhere

Teaching at Royal Holloway has included Shakespeare, Renaissance literature, early modern bodies, poetry, adaptation and Shakespeare on screen, alongside postgraduate teaching and dissertation supervision.

I have also contributed invited teaching and supervision at other institutions.

Independent work

Independent Supervision, Tuition and Funding Applications

I offer independent one-to-one supervision and tuition for undergraduate and postgraduate students, doctoral researchers and independent scholars working in Shakespeare, early modern literature, theatre and performance, adaptation, Holocaust and memory studies, and related areas of literary and cultural criticism.

Sessions can support the development of research questions, dissertation and thesis planning, close reading, argument and structure, responding to feedback, and discussion of work in progress. I can also advise on MA and doctoral research proposals.

I offer guidance on research funding and fellowship applications, including project design, framing, case for support, work plans and application strategy. My own research has attracted approximately £500,000 in competitive funding, including major fellowship support.

Independent supervision and tuition are intended to supplement, rather than replace, formal supervision provided by a university.
