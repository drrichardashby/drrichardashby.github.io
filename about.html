Biography

Richard Ashby is an Honorary Research Associate in the Department of English at Royal Holloway, University of London. He previously held a British Academy Postdoctoral Fellowship at King’s College London and has taught undergraduate and postgraduate students at King’s and Royal Holloway, including dissertation supervision.

His first monograph, King Lear ‘After’ Auschwitz: Shakespeare, Appropriation and Theatres of Catastrophe in Post-War British Drama, was published by Edinburgh University Press in 2020. His current research includes books on Shakespeare and Holocaust writing and on Vladimir Jankélévitch, Macbeth and the Holocaust, alongside a new project on the literary and philosophical afterlives of the old mole.

His research has attracted approximately £500,000 in competitive funding. Alongside academic research and teaching, he works on public-facing and creative-critical projects involving theatre, visual art, interviews, digital scholarship and writing for non-specialist audiences.
