About

I am a literary and cultural scholar specialising in Shakespeare and its post-Holocaust afterlives across literature, theatre, film, television and philosophy.

Profile

I am an Honorary Research Associate in the Department of English at Royal Holloway, University of London. I previously held a British Academy Postdoctoral Fellowship at King’s College London and have taught at King’s and Royal Holloway.

My work crosses Shakespeare studies, Holocaust studies, theatre and performance, adaptation, memory, philosophy and intellectual history.

Public work

My public-facing work includes research collaboration, performance projects, interviews and writing for non-specialist audiences, including work with Deborah Leiser-Moore and Tom de Freston.

I have written for History Today and contributed to the Digital Levi project.
