About

I am a literary scholar specialising in Shakespeare and its post-Holocaust afterlives across literature, theatre, film, television and philosophy.

Profile

I am an Honorary Research Associate in the Department of English at Royal Holloway, University of London. I previously held a British Academy Postdoctoral Fellowship at King’s College London and have taught at King’s and Royal Holloway.

My work crosses Shakespeare studies, Holocaust studies, theatre and performance, adaptation, memory, philosophy and intellectual history.

Public engagement

I also work beyond conventional academic publication through creative-critical projects, research expertise for artists, public writing and digital scholarship.
