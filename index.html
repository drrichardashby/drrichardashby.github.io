Literary scholar

Richard Ashby

I am an interdisciplinary literary scholar working on Shakespeare and his afterlives, particularly in post-Holocaust culture. I investigate the way writers and artists use Shakespeare to navigate crisis and catastrophe. I have also worked on the history of Shakespeare in performance and criticism, Shakespeare on film and TV, and Shakespeare and psychoanalytic theory, among other areas.

Honorary Research Associate, Department of English, Royal Holloway, University of London.
